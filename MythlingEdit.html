Battle placement
Drag the Mythling to move it; drag its shadow to move the shadow alone. Arrow keys nudge the Mythling (Shift = bigger steps), Alt+arrows the shadow. Each side keeps its own values. Copy sends the whole block.
Starter card placement
Drag the Mythling to move it; drag its shadow to move the shadow alone. Arrow keys nudge the Mythling (Shift = bigger steps), Alt+arrows the shadow. The shadow is always painted behind the Mythling. Copy sends the block for src/ui/screens.js.
Placement
Drag the gold crosshair to the creature's feet, or type the numbers. That exact pixel is placed on the ground line, so the picture is never cut to fit. Undo walks back through your changes; Reset returns to where the picture opened (the saved placement when there is one, otherwise the auto-detected feet).
Picture
The picture is used as-is. Only the numbers above change where it lands.
