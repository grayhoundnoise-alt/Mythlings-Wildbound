Mythlings: Wildbound

Creature Design Bible. Every frame on this page is rendered live by src/render/creatures.js — the exact code the game runs. Nothing here is concept art that the game cannot reproduce: what you see is what you catch.

Design rules enforced by the renderer: a readable silhouette built from 3–5 signature forms, layered anatomy (haunch, chest, neck, muzzle, articulated paws), a species-specific eye shape with six playable expressions, element expressed through anatomy rather than hue, and evolution that grows the body and the signature feature instead of scaling the sprite.

All creatures, names, markings and colour scripts are original work for Mythlings: Wildbound. Lv.60 / Lv.80 stages are shown as locked because they are defined in data but deliberately unreachable in this build.
